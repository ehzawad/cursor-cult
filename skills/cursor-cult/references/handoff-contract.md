# Handoff contract

Every role handoff should be independently useful and compact enough for the parent or builder to consume without rereading the entire investigation.

## Required fields

1. **Role and mandate** — the exact question answered.
2. **Verdict or recommendation** — one paragraph at most.
3. **Evidence** — paths, symbols, line ranges, commands, outputs, specifications, or first-party sources.
4. **Findings** — ordered by impact, not discovery order.
5. **Implications** — what the parent or builder should do with the findings.
6. **Uncertainty** — assumptions, missing evidence, and confidence.

## Finding shape

For each material finding:

- Claim
- Evidence
- Impact
- Recommended action or decisive check

A role must say “nothing material found” when its lens produces no finding. Silence is ambiguous.
